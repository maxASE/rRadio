                        Your account

                        Logged in as . You can make channels of your own; switch one to public under its Channel settings so everyone else can listen to it too.

                        Users

                        Everyone with a login can make their own channels, with their own stations, programs, spots and files. A channel they switch to public shows up for everyone else to listen to, but only its owner (and you) can change it.

                        No UI_PASSWORD is set, so there is no login and everyone is the admin. Set one in .env to use user accounts.

                        Add a user

                        Settings

                        Stream formats are switched on and off under Admin › Streams.

                        New video uploads and podcast episodes wait while a program is on. To let them interrupt one, open it under Schedule › Edit.

                        Music server For stations and programs that play your own music: Navidrome, Gonic, Airsonic, Ampache, LMS, Funkwhale or any other Subsonic server. Funkwhale wants the address ending in /api/subsonic.

                        Apps and devices

                        Pair the rRadio app by scanning a QR code. Each phone gets its own key, which you can limit to some channels or remove at any time. The app can control your own channels and listen to public ones.

                        No UI_PASSWORD is set, so anyone on your network can still use rRadio without a key. Set one in .env to make device limits count.

                        Channels None ticked means all channels. A listen-and-control app can skip, queue, cut in and change the station on these channels, but not change settings, sources or the schedule.

                        Scan this with the rRadio app, or enter the code by hand.
